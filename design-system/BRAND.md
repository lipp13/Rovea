# Rovea — Brand Identity & Art Direction Specification

> **Product:** Rovea Mobile Application (React Native + Expo)  
> **Category:** Premium Travel Companion & Itinerary Planner  
> **Visual System Alignment:** Warm Editorial Minimal (`design-system/MASTER.md`)  
> **Source of Truth:** `design-system/BRAND.md`

---

## 1. Brand Positioning

Rovea is an intentional, editorial travel companion designed for discerning modern travelers—young explorers, solo wanderers, couples, and small groups who view travel not as a checklist of tourist traps, but as a rich narrative of discovery, culture, and human connection.

Where traditional travel apps are chaotic, hyper-commercialized booking engines filled with intrusive pop-ups and endless star ratings, Rovea acts as a serene, curated sanctuary. It bridges the aesthetic elegance of high-end print travel journalism (*Cereal*, *Kinfolk*) with the seamless utility of an Apple-grade mobile itinerary planner.

---

## 2. Brand Promise

> **"To transform trip planning from a fragmented chore into an inspiring, serene prelude to your next great journey."**

Rovea promises:
* **Clarity over Chaos:** Effortless organization of itineraries, places, expenses, and packing without administrative friction.
* **Inspiration over Noise:** Curated, image-first discovery that respects the traveler's intelligence and aesthetic standards.
* **Confidence everywhere:** Offline-ready, thumb-friendly navigation that keeps you present in the moment rather than staring at your screen.

---

## 3. Brand Personality

The Rovea brand identity is defined by five core traits:

* **Intentional:** Every detail has a purpose; no extraneous visual clutter or artificial gamification.
* **Warm & Human:** Grounded in earthy, sunlight-baked tones (`#FBF9F5` Sandstone, `#C25E38` Terracotta) rather than cold clinical tech blues.
* **Understated Elegance:** Speaks with quiet confidence through refined serif headlines, generous whitespace, and tactile imagery.
* **Curious & Thoughtful:** Inspires deep exploration, local nuance, and meaningful wanderlust.
* **Reliable Companion:** Calm, precise, and dependable when navigating foreign streets or managing daily budgets.

---

## 4. Brand Voice

Rovea’s voice is **literary yet approachable, concise yet evocative**. It reads like an experienced travel journalist who speaks with warmth and precision.

| Voice Attribute | What We Are | What We Are NOT |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tone** | Inviting, calm, articulate | Hype-filled, pushy, corporate |
| **Language** | Evocative, sensory, clear | Slang-heavy, dry, technical |
| **Pacing** | Measured, thoughtful | Urgent, aggressive, spammy |
| **Perspective** | A knowledgeable fellow traveler | A pushy travel agent or algorithm |

## 5. Brand Tone Matrix

Rovea adapts its tone dynamically depending on the user's emotional state in the travel journey:

```
[Inspiration / Discover] ──► Warm, Evocative, Poetic
[Trip Planning / Setup]  ──► Clear, Helpful, Structured
[Active Journey Mode]    ──► Efficient, Direct, Reassuring
[Post-Trip / Memories]   ──► Nostalgic, Celebratory, Soft
```

* **Onboarding & Discovery:** Evocative & Inspiring (*"Where will curiosity take you next?"*)
* **Itinerary & Expenses:** Precise & Frictionless (*"Day 3 added to Kyoto. 4 places scheduled."*)
* **Errors & Delays:** Reassuring & Solutions-Focused (*"You're offline. Your trip data is safely saved locally."*)

## 7. Logo Concept Directions

The logo for Rovea must convey movement, horizon, and place without reverting to cliché travel icons (airplanes, globes, location pins, compasses, or suitcases).

Key abstract themes explored:
* **The Horizon Line:** The threshold between where you are and where you are going.
* **The Continuous Path:** A fluid, unbroken journey that returns enriched.
* **The Meridian / Intersection:** The meeting point of spatial location, time, and memory.
* **The Architectural Arch:** Passage through new cultures and landscapes.

---

## 8. Wordmark Direction

The Rovea wordmark is set in a customized, high-contrast serif font based on **Playfair Display / Newsreader**, modified with:
* Custom refined optical tracking for uppercase display.
* Tapered terminal curves on the capital **'R'** suggesting a gentle path curvature.
* Open counter spaces in 'o' and 'e' to maintain high clarity even at small mobile header sizes.
* Paired with a geometric sans-serif sub-label for context (`ROVEA — Travel Planning`).

---

## 9. Logo Mark Concepts (5 Distinct Concepts)

### Concept 1: The Horizon Threshold (*"The Horizon 'R'"*)
* **Visual Description:** A bold, minimal capital serif **'R'** where the horizontal crossbar extends subtly into an unbroken fine horizon line (`1.5px`), separating a warm golden semi-circle sun from the ground stroke.
* **Design Rationale:** Captures the sunrise over new destinations. The intersection of the classic editorial letterform with a clean geometric line symbolizes crossing into new territory.
* **Mood:** Classic, literary, timeless.

### Concept 2: The Continuous Loop (*"The Traveler's Path"*) — RECOMMENDED
* **Visual Description:** An abstract, continuous single-line mark featuring a soft arch that flows into a grounded diagonal stroke, forming a subtle combination of the letter **'r'** and an architectural archway.
* **Design Rationale:** Symbolizes seamless movement—starting a journey, exploring open spaces, and returning home. The arch represents passage into new cities without relying on literal location pins.
* **Mood:** Modern, minimal, warm, architectural.

### Concept 3: The Meridian Point (*"The Convergence"*)
* **Visual Description:** Two intersecting geometric hairline curves forming a subtle cardinal intersection point with an offset terracotta filled focal dot (`#C25E38`).
* **Design Rationale:** Represents the precise moment of arrival when a traveler steps into a new city. It references spatial navigation in an abstract, elevated manner.
* **Mood:** Precision, calm navigation, technical elegance.

### Concept 4: The Topographic Contour (*"The Terrain"*)
* **Visual Description:** Three organic, parallel hairline curves that gently wave together, creating a stylized contour line map pattern inside a warm circle frame.
* **Design Rationale:** Celebrates physical landscape, elevation changes, and geographic exploration without looking like a busy street map.
* **Mood:** Organic, tactile, nature-focused.

### Concept 5: The Bookmark Journal (*"The Travelogue"*)
* **Visual Description:** A clean geometric ribbon/bookmark silhouette integrated into the right leg of a minimal serif **'R'**.
* **Design Rationale:** Directly references Rovea’s editorial magazine DNA—saving favorite spots, curating daily itineraries, and preserving memories like a prized travel book.
* **Mood:** Editorial, curated, narrative.

---

### Strongest Recommended Direction: **Concept 2 — The Continuous Loop ("The Traveler's Path")**

**Why Concept 2 fits Rovea best:**
1. **Unforgettable & Timeless:** It avoids all travel clichés while retaining an intuitive association with journeys and architectural entryways.
2. **Mobile Screen Precision:** Scales flawlessly down to a 16x16px app icon or favicons without losing legibility.
3. **Harmonizes with UI:** Pairs perfectly with the **Warmed Sandstone** palette and serif typography established in `design-system/MASTER.md`.

## 10. Photography Art Direction

Images are the visual core of Rovea. Photography must feel **cinematic, authentic, and atmospheric**—never staged or stock-like.

### Core Visual Principles
* **Natural Light Only:** Golden hour, soft morning haze, overcast moody daylight. No harsh studio flashes or artificial HDR saturations.
* **Human Presence & Scale:** Include human figures naturally—viewed from behind, looking out over a valley, walking through a quiet alleyway—inviting the user to step into the scene.
* **Tactile & Environmental:** Focus on textures—worn cobblestone streets, linen curtains in a sunlit hotel room, steam rising from a morning coffee.

---

## 11. Image Composition Rules

* **Aspect Ratios:**
  * Destination Cover Cards: Vertical Editorial `4:5`
  * Hero Headers: Landscape `16:9`
  * Spot Thumbnails: Square `1:1` with `8px` rounded corners
* **Gradient Overlay Rule:**
  * Every image with text overlay **MUST** feature a 2-stop linear scrim:
    * Top text: `rgba(0,0,0,0.45)` fading to `transparent` over 30% of height.
    * Bottom text: `rgba(0,0,0,0.70)` fading up to `transparent` over 45% of height.
* **Color Temperature:** Warm (+5 to +10 tint towards amber/earthy hues); avoid cold blue filters unless depicting arctic weather.

---

## 12. Illustration Direction

Rovea uses **restrained, fine-line architectural illustrations** exclusively for empty states and onboarding graphics:

* **Style:** 1.5px warm hairline stroke (`#78716C` in Light Mode, `#A8A29E` in Dark Mode) with subtle terracotta accent tints (`#C25E38`).
* **Subject Matter:** Abstract streetscapes, coffee cups on café tables, luggage tags, passports, mountain outlines, and sun horizons.
* **Avoid:** Flat corporate vector 3D figures, cartoonish blob characters, or bright colorful pop art.

---

## 13. Icon Personality

* **Library:** **Phosphor Icons** (`@phosphor-icons/react-native`)
* **Weight Discipline:**
  * Inactive / Default Controls: **Regular** (`strokeWidth = 1.5`)
  * Active / Selected State: **Fill** or **Bold**
* **Tone:** Clean, geometric, rounded joins (`strokeLinejoin="round"`).
* **Strict Avoidance:** No emojis used as structural UI icons or tab labels.

---

## 14. Brand Storytelling Principles

1. **Curate, Don't Overwhelm:** Present destinations through narrative collections (e.g. *"48 Hours in Kyoto’s Artisan Quarter"*), not chaotic lists of 500 places.
2. **Context is Everything:** Highlight weather, local time, and walking distances alongside place details.
3. **Respect the Traveler's Autonomy:** Give travelers clean tools to drag, reorder, and customize their itineraries effortlessly.
